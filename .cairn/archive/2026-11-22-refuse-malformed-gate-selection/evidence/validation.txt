Review follow-up (2026-11-22):

An independent review found two P2 defects. The duplicate diagnostic echoed the
whole command, so two identical multi-megabyte entries made the issue (copied
into both the summary and the failed check) grow with the input. Uniqueness used
a linear scan per entry, which is quadratic for a long well-formed list. The fix
reports `[i] duplicates entry [j]` with indices only and checks uniqueness with
a map from entry to its first index; the vector keeps the declared order. The
review also found that the survey omitted ChaosControl, now added.

## Why

Cairn only needs Radicle's Git-backed Collaborative Object format and signature primitives. The current adapter instead links the umbrella `radicle` standard library, loads Radicle profiles and identity documents, opens Radicle-managed storage, and exposes node connect/fetch synchronization. That makes node, discovery, seeding, networking, profile layout, and repository-identity policy part of Cairn's build and public collaboration surface even though they are outside the desired product boundary.

## What Changes

- Replace the umbrella `radicle` dependency with direct `radicle-cob`, `radicle-crypto`, `radicle-oid`, Git, and ref-format primitives.
- Provide a Cairn-owned thin Git COB store over one explicit repository path while preserving signed COB entries, operation DAG evaluation, custom type identity, and exact action bytes.
- Replace Radicle profile and identity-document authority with one explicit keystore and immutable creator/delegate authority recorded in the root change state.
- Expose the local adapter as `radicle-cob.v1` and `cairn cob`; remove Radicle home, RID, node, peer, seeding, connect/fetch, convergence, and `collaboration sync` behavior.
- Preserve the portable collaboration backend and the pure deterministic collaboration reducer.

## Impact

- **Files**: workspace manifests/lockfile generated by Cargo, `crates/cairn-core`, `crates/cairn-radicle`, `crates/cairn-cli`, Radicle transport tests, `flake.nix`, README/operator docs, workflow skill, and lifecycle specifications.
- **Testing**: baseline and post-change core/adapter/CLI tests, direct COB positive and negative integration, a dependency audit proving no umbrella `radicle` or `radicle-node` surface remains, workspace formatting/tests/Clippy, Cairn lifecycle and traceability gates, release-readiness, self-dogfood, and full flake validation.
